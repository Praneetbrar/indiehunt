# Compare IndieHunt pricing

Read https://indiehunt.io/pricing.md before recommending a plan. Do not invent a paid nofollow tier.

## Tiers

- Free — $0. Badge required. Nofollow by default. 15 slots per week. Weekly top 3 can earn dofollow.
- Premium — $29 one-time. Guaranteed dofollow. No badge requirement. Unlimited slots. Site-wide ads during the 7-day live week, launch story, 9 Launch Kit PNGs, and social posts.

Humans check out at https://indiehunt.io/pricing and submit at https://indiehunt.io/submit. There is no contact-sales step for either public tier.
